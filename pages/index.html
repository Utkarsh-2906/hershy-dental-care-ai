OUR STORY

Personalized Dental Care in Hershey, PA

When you visit any of our offices, your smile is our top priority. Our entire team is dedicated to providing you with the personalized, gentle care that you deserve. Dr. Bhargav Patel and our entire team proudly offer Hershey location to provide our patients with quality and comprehensive dental care. We offer a variety of dental procedures including teeth whitening, extractions, dental implants, root canals, crowns, bridges, veneers, dentures, and Invisalign. Whether you and your family need a complete dental exam, a full- mouth restoration, or anything in between, our team of highly qualified doctors will give you something to smile about.

Meet DR. Bhargav Patel, DMD

Dr. Bhargav Patel is a native of Dauphin County. He graduated from University of Medicine and Dentistry NJ, in 2010. Dr. Patel worked as a dental assistant throughout school, placing him far above his classmates in accumulating practical experience in the dental field. Dr. Patel is constantly on a quest for knowledge, and he averages six times the required continuing education credits mandated for license renewal. However, what really separates Dr. Patel from other dentists is his passion and love for the profession, and his strong desire to please every patient in his care.
